Asian Waffles

Hong Kong - Bubble Waffles

The bubble waffle is a very famous street snack from Hong Kong! Other names for this treat include egg waffle, Hong Kong waffle, and gai daan jai!
The reason why this waffle is called an egg waffle is because the batter is mainly egg-based, and the texture formed by the griddle are egg-shaped!
Bubble waffles used to be eaten plain, but modern ways to eat it include with ice cream, gelato, and caramel sauce!
I had a bubble waffle a LONG time ago at the mall, but I remember it being so fluffy and amazing. Definitely a 10/10!

Vietnam - Pandan Waffles

As I was researching this waffle type, I was very intrigued by the color! Apparently, pandan is a green plant, and its paste is used for this waffle!
Coconut is often added into the waffle, and the use of traditional pandan, which tastes like "Asian vanilla", makes it even better!
This waffle is also known as Bánh Kẹp Lá Dứa!
Just from looking at this waffle, I feel like I need to eat it instantly! :D
