The History of Waffles

Below is the history of how waffles originated!

  • Waffles began its journey in Ancient Greece, where the Athenians made flat cakes called obelios.
  • Tastier prototypes of waffles arose in the Middle Ages. In fact, this version was said to have hearts and the coat of arms engraved.
  • Today, waffles are a breakfast staple in America and have spread via stimulus diffusion to many parts of the world!
