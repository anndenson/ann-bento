European Waffles

The Netherlands - The Stroopwafel

The stroopwafel is an extremely popular type of waffle that expanded from just its Dutch origins.
It started as a siroopwafel, or a syrup waffle made from leftover cookie scraps. Due to this, it was considered a "poor man's cookie".
Now, however, it is highly regarded as one of the tastiest types of waffles! Served in flights and cafes, these elegant waffles have definitely made their mark.
Personally, I had these waffles before as a snack during a flight, and I would definitely say they are a 9.5/10!


Information Source: https://mellysstroopwafels.nl/blog/history-of-the-stroopwafel

Norway - Hjertevafler (Heart Waffles)

The Norwegian waffle is quite memorable, especially due to its lovely heart shape!
These delightful waffles are more than just sweet treats, though. They are recognized as a symbol of kos, or togetherness, a huge part of Norway's culture.
I have yet to try this type of waffle, but it certainly looks extremely interesting!
